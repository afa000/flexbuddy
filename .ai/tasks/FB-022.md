## Objective

A driver who shares Flex screenshots to FlexBuddy should get all of them in the import review, including when they
had to sign in first, and should be able to find out that sharing exists.

## Files to read

- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/static/sw.js`: `receiveSharedScreenshot`, `removeStaleShares`, `SHARE_CACHE`,
  `SHARE_MAX_AGE_MS`.
- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/static/js/app.js`: `importSharedScreenshot`, `takeSharedFile`, `pickScreenshots`,
  `SHARE_INBOX`, `SHARED_UNAVAILABLE`.
- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/static/js/pwa.js` (`USER_CACHE_PREFIXES`), `account.js` (`guardedSignOut`).
- `templates/login.html`, `templates/shifts.html` (import guidance).
- `PageController.sharedScreenshotWithoutServiceWorker`, `PageControllerTest`, `pwa-language.test.js`.

## Required behavior

1. **Every shared screenshot.** The service worker keeps every shared PNG/JPEG file (up to 10), each under
   `/share-inbox/<id>/<n>`, and the import screen opens all of them: one file is today's single review, two to ten
   start an FB-021 batch. More than 10 keep nothing and the import screen says "Share up to 10 screenshots at a
   time." A share kept under the old single key `/share-inbox/<id>` is still read.
2. **Shared while signed out.** The Sign in page says "Sign in to review the screenshot you shared." while a share
   younger than 10 minutes is waiting. After sign-in, the app opens the import screen with it, as if it had just been
   shared, and a toast says it is ready to review. Nothing is saved without the driver's press, as now.
3. **Old shares.** Shares older than 10 minutes are deleted when the app or the sign-in page loads, and never opened.
   Sign-out still deletes all shares.
4. **Say sharing exists.** On Android, the import card adds "On Android, you can also share screenshots to
   FlexBuddy from Photos." Other devices don't show it.
5. The share inbox logic shared by the app and the sign-in page lives in a new `static/js/share-inbox.js` with Node
   tests.

## Out of scope

- The Android wrapper (`android/`), its intent filter and share target: no change, no new Play upload.
- Server endpoints and security config; the no-service-worker fallback stays as it is.
- Sharing other file types.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Several shared screenshots all reach the import review (batch for 2 to 10); more than 10 are refused with a
      message; nothing is saved without a press.
- [ ] A share made while signed out is announced on Sign in and opened after sign-in; one older than 10 minutes is
      deleted, not opened.
- [ ] Sign-out still deletes waiting shares; no other account's image can open (shares are per device, cleared on
      sign-out, and expire after 10 minutes).
- [ ] The Android-only sharing tip shows on Android only; English and Spanish.

## Security and regression risks

- Shared images stay on the device (Cache Storage) and are never sent before the import screen reads them; the
  10-minute limit and sign-out clearing are unchanged.
- The Sign in page reads the share inbox only to decide whether to show the note; it never shows the image.
- Both catalogs; `sw.js` version list gets the new script.
