## Decision on payment tracking

Payout-level verification (the existing Payouts sheet: what landed against what a period's blocks earned) is enough
for this task. No per-block paid/unpaid field is added; "completed" is never treated as "paid". If per-block payment
tracking is wanted later, it is a separate high-risk task.

## Objective

After a block, the details that make the figures right sit in four places: the finish sheet (actual times, miles or
odometer), the missing-miles reminder, the Expenses page (tolls, parking), and Payout history. A driver has to know
each one exists. One "Review this block" checklist should show what is done, what is missing, what is explicitly
zero and what is optional, and open the right existing screen for each.

## Required behavior

1. **Checklist sheet** (`#blockReviewModal`) for a completed block, four rows, each with a state and an action:
   - **Actual times**: recorded ("9:02 AM – 12:48 PM · 3h 46m on the clock") or "Not recorded · optional". Add
     times / Change opens the existing finish sheet (details mode).
   - **Miles**: a number ("42.0 mi"), explicitly zero ("No miles driven") or missing ("Not recorded"). Missing
     offers Add miles (finish sheet) and No miles (the existing explicit-zero save, which also ends the reminder).
   - **Tolls & parking**: linked toll/parking expenses ("$4.50 in 2 expenses") or "None linked · optional". "Add a
     toll or parking" opens the Expenses form with the date set to the block's day, Toll chosen and the block
     already linked, so it isn't re-entered.
   - **Payout**: the payout whose period covers the block's day, from the pay periods: upcoming ("Comes with the Tue
     Nov 10 payout"), not checked ("Tue Nov 10 payout not checked yet"), matched, short ("... was $3.00 short") or
     more than earned; a block older than the loaded periods says "Check it in Payout history". Not checked, short
     and unknown offer Check payout, which opens the existing Payouts sheet.
   The heading says how many rows need attention (missing miles, an unchecked or short payout), or "All checked".
   Optional rows are never counted as missing. Nothing is saved by the sheet itself except No miles.
2. **Entry points**:
   - Home: a "Your last block" card when the newest completed block is from today or yesterday, with its station,
     day and "2 to check" / "All checked", and a Review button. No other trigger, no notification.
   - Edit dialog of a completed block: a "Review this block" button beside Linked expenses. With unsaved changes in
     the dialog it says "Save or cancel your changes first." instead of leaving them behind.
3. Scheduled, cancelled and forfeited blocks have no checklist (their figures don't count until confirmed).
4. The sheet loads the block again by id, so it shows what is saved, and only the driver's own block (the endpoints
   are owner-scoped).

## Out of scope

- Per-block payment tracking, payout changes, new endpoints, migrations, notifications.
- Changing the finish sheet, the Payouts sheet or the expense form themselves.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The checklist opens the correct owned block from Home and from the edit dialog.
- [ ] Missing, explicitly zero and optional states read differently; optional rows aren't counted as to-check.
- [ ] No miles saves an explicit zero and clears the missing-miles reminder; times/miles open the finish sheet with
      the other details intact.
- [ ] A toll can be added already linked to the block, without choosing it again.
- [ ] Payout review opens the existing Payouts sheet; completed is not called paid.
- [ ] English and Spanish; phone width; no horizontal scroll.
