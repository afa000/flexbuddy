# Task: FB-009 - Say when estimated net is too high because blocks have no miles

Status: Ready for implementation
Risk: medium (labels next to earnings figures; the figures and their calculation must not change)
Implementer: Antigravity
Review: fresh Claude Code session (Opus), review-task skill

## Objective

With standard mileage, the vehicle cost of a block comes only from its miles. A driver who hasn't logged miles sees
estimated net equal to gross: Reports says **Est. net $335.00 · After deductions · 100.0% margin**, and Home says
**Est. net / hr $24.00/hr** when gross is also $24.00/hr. Fuel they logged doesn't lower it either, which is correct for
standard mileage but isn't explained. A driver reads this as "I keep everything I earn". FlexBuddy should say when net
is overstated because of missing miles, and say why fuel doesn't count.

## Current behavior

Checked against `main` at 5b45efe, and reproduced on `localhost` on October 30, 2026 with a new test account and
three completed blocks with no miles:

- **Reports, All time:**
  - Earned $335.00;
  - **Est. net $335.00, "After deductions · 100.0% margin"**;
  - Avg hourly $25.77, "$25.77/hr est. net";
  - Miles 0.0, "$0.00 mileage cost".
- **Home, this week:** Est. net / hr **$24.00/hr**, captioned "estimate". That's the same as gross.
- **Expenses:** I logged $40 of fuel. Reports' Expenses card shows $40.00, and Est. net stays $335.00. That's correct
  under standard mileage, because the rate covers fuel. The Expenses page only says "Vehicle costs use standard mileage
  at $0.70/mi · Change".
- **Block evaluator:** already says "Log miles on your blocks to include vehicle cost." (`js.evaluate.logMiles`).
  Leave it as is.

## Required behavior

1. **Count the blocks without miles.** Add `blocksWithoutMiles` to `NetEarningsResult`: the number of shifts in the
   calculation that count toward hours and have `getMiles() == null`. Every other value in the result stays exactly the
   same.
2. **Expose it in the statistics.** Add `blocksWithoutMiles` to `ShiftStatisticsResponse`, filled from the result.
3. **Reports Est. net card.** When the vehicle cost method is standard mileage and `blocksWithoutMiles > 0`, the line
   under Est. net (`#netMargin`) reads:
   - English: "1 block has no miles, so this is too high" / "3 blocks have no miles, so this is too high";
   - Spanish: "1 bloque no tiene millas, así que esto es demasiado alto" / "3 bloques no tienen millas, así que esto es
     demasiado alto".

   Use new plural keys `js.app.netMissingMiles.one` and `.other` with `tn`, as `js.home.blocksNoMiles` does. Otherwise
   it keeps today's "After deductions · X% margin". The amount itself doesn't change.
4. **Home Est. net / hr tile.** Under the same conditions, for this week's statistics, the caption under `#weekNetHourly`
   changes from "estimate" to "missing miles" (new key `js.home.missingMiles`, Spanish "faltan millas"). Otherwise it
   stays "estimate".
5. **Actual expenses.** With the actual-expenses method, miles don't affect net, so neither message appears.
6. **Expenses page.** With standard mileage, add one sentence under the cost note: "Fuel and maintenance are covered by
   the mileage rate, so they don't lower your estimated net." Use new key `expenses.fuelCoveredByMileage`; Spanish: "El
   combustible y el mantenimiento están cubiertos por la tarifa por milla, así que no reducen su ganancia neta estimada."
   Hide it with actual expenses. It updates when the method changes, the same way `#expenseCostMethod` does.
7. **Testable logic.** Put the rule (standard mileage and a count above zero gives the count, otherwise none) in a small
   function exported on `window.flexbuddyHome` with the existing functions, and use it in both places. `app.js` can call
   `window.flexbuddyHome`. Cover it with Node tests.
