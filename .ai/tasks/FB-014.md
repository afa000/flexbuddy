# Task: FB-014 - Say what a saved expense does to estimated net

Status: Ready for implementation
Risk: medium (feedback text next to earnings; no calculation changes)
Implementer: Claude Code (Opus). The plan names Antigravity; the owner handed wave 2 to this session on
November 6, 2026.
Review: fresh Claude Code session (Opus), review-task skill

Source: DX-04 in `docs/driver-experience-plan.md`.

## Objective

Every expense save says "Net earnings have been recalculated." (`js.account.netRecalculated`). Under standard mileage,
fuel and maintenance don't change estimated net at all, because the mileage rate already covers them, and the
Expenses page itself says so in a note. A driver who logs $40 of fuel, reads "recalculated", and sees net unchanged is
left confused. The message should say what the expense actually does, and the form should say it before saving.

## Files to read

- `flexbuddy/src/main/java/com/angel/flexbuddy/service/NetEarningsCalculator.java`: `calculate` (fuel and
  maintenance are the vehicle cost only under actual expenses; toll, parking and other are always out of pocket).
- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/static/js/app.js`: `loadExpenses` (reads `/account/settings`), `editExpense`,
  `resetExpenseForm`, `saveExpense` (the toast), `deleteExpense`.
- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/templates/shifts.html`: the expense form (`#expenseCategory`, the `#expenseShift`
  `field-hint` pattern) and the cost notes (`#expenseCostMethod`, `#expenseFuelNote`).
- Tests: `NetEarningsCalculatorTest`, `src/test/js/load-script.js` and an existing Node test for the pattern.
- Catalog keys: `js.account.netRecalculated` (also used by `account.js` after a settings save; leave that one),
  `js.app.expenseAdded`, `js.app.expenseUpdated`, `expenses.fuelCoveredByMileage`, `expenses.linkItToAShift`.

## Current behavior

- Adding or editing any expense shows "Expense added" / "Expense updated" with "Net earnings have been recalculated."
- The form gives no hint about the chosen category's effect. The page's cost note explains fuel only under standard
  mileage, below the form.

## Required behavior

1. **One rule, matching the calculator.** A new script `static/js/expense-effect.js` exports `effectKey(category,
   method)`:
   - standard mileage and `FUEL` or `MAINTENANCE`: `js.expenseEffect.coveredByMileage`;
   - any other known category under standard mileage, or any category under actual expenses:
     `js.expenseEffect.lowersNet`;
   - unknown method (settings not loaded) or unknown category: `undefined`.
2. **Hint under Category.** `#expenseCategoryHint` (a `field-hint`, linked by `aria-describedby`) shows the text for
   the selected category and the account's method. It updates when the category changes, when an expense is opened
   for editing, when the form resets, and when settings load. It's empty while the method is unknown.
3. **Save message.** After an add or edit, the toast keeps its title and its message becomes the effect text for the
   saved category, or `js.expenseEffect.saved` ("Your expense totals are up to date.") when the method is unknown.
   The offline-queued message is unchanged.
4. **Texts.**
   - `js.expenseEffect.coveredByMileage`: "Your mileage rate already covers this, so it doesn't lower your estimated
     net."
   - `js.expenseEffect.lowersNet`: "This lowers your estimated net."
   - `js.expenseEffect.saved`: "Your expense totals are up to date."
   Spanish in the same group.

## Out of scope

- The calculation, the cost notes already on the page, delete/restore behavior and messages, the account settings
  toast in `account.js`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The hint and the save message match the calculator for all five categories under both methods.
- [ ] Changing the category, opening an expense to edit, or resetting the form refreshes the hint.
- [ ] No message says net was recalculated or lowered for fuel or maintenance under standard mileage.
- [ ] Edit, delete and restore keep their current behavior; no figure changes.
- [ ] English and Spanish; `SpanishMessagesTest` passes.

## Validation

- Node: table-driven `effectKey` test over 5 categories x 2 methods, plus unknown method and category.
- Java: a table-driven `NetEarningsCalculatorTest` that saves one expense of each category under each method and
  asserts whether net drops by its amount, using the same table, so the two stay in step.
- `.\scripts\verify.ps1 -TaskId FB-014`.
- Browser at phone width with a local test account: fuel and toll under standard mileage (hint, toast, net
  unchanged / lowered), the same under actual expenses, edit an expense, English and Spanish.
- No migration.

## Security and regression risks

- Text only; no endpoint or data change. Both catalogs.
