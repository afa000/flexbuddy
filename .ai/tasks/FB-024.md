# Task: FB-024 - Android version 5: the driver experience changes as a Play update

Status: Ready for implementation
Risk: low (wrapper version only; no shell, package, signing-key or asset-link change)
Implementer: Claude Code (Opus) for the build and notes; the owner signs and uploads (keystore and Play Console are
owner-only). Requested by the owner on November 7, 2026: "updating the app on play console to reflect the changes".
Review: owner check of the built APK details and notes before upload

## Objective

Everything from the driver experience plan (FB-011 to FB-023) is live on the web, so the Play app already shows it.
The closed test still needs updates Play can see, with notes saying what changed (Google's October 24 reply asked for
"updates to your app"). Version 5 brings these changes to testers as an update, like version 4 did.

## Files to read

- `android/twa-manifest.json` (`appVersionCode`, `appVersionName`, `appVersion`), `android/README.md` ("Update the
  wrapper", "Build a signed release"), `android/CHANGELOG-android.md` (version 4 entry),
  `android/closed-test-round-2.md` ("Release notes").

## Required behavior

1. `appVersionCode` 5, `appVersionName` "5", `appVersion` "5" in `twa-manifest.json`; `bubblewrap update
   --skipVersionUpgrade`; restore what `update` changed without reason (icons, splash, `store_icon.png`,
   `shortcuts.xml`, whitespace-only Java/XML), keep the version in `app/build.gradle` and `manifest-checksum.txt`.
2. `bubblewrap build --skipSigning`; the APK reads package `com.angel.flexbuddy`, `versionCode='5'
   versionName='5'`, `minSdkVersion 24`, and the same three launcher shortcuts.
3. Release notes for version 5 in English and Spanish (500 characters or fewer each) in
   `closed-test-round-2.md`, naming the changes in the driver's words. No tester feedback is logged, so they don't
   credit testers.
4. A version 5 entry in `CHANGELOG-android.md`, completed by the owner's signing and upload.

## Out of scope

- Any shell change, package name, upload key, Play app signing, `assetlinks.json`, store listing or screenshots.
- Signing, uploading or sending for review (owner).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The unsigned APK and AAB are version 5 (5) with the same package, SDK levels and shortcuts as version 4.
- [ ] Only the version files and `manifest-checksum.txt` change under `android/app` (plus anything `update` needs and
      is explained).
- [ ] English and Spanish notes fit in 500 characters.

## Validation

- `aapt2 dump badging` on the unsigned APK; `git status` under `android/`.
- After the owner signs: `apksigner verify --print-certs` shows the upload key `A3:EC:82:…:FE:37`; `jarsigner -verify`
  on the bundle.
- After Play approves: the emulator's Play app updates to 5 (5) and opens full screen.

## Security and regression risks

- The keystore and its password stay with the owner; no password in commands, files or chat.
- Both asset-link fingerprints stay; the package name and upload key don't change.
