# Task: FB-020 - Phone-friendly import instructions and a shorter upload card

Status: Ready for implementation
Risk: low (import screen copy and layout; no data or OCR changes)
Implementer: Claude Code (Opus). The plan names OpenCode + local model; the owner handed wave 3 to this session on
November 6, 2026, with approval to merge once the fresh review is clean and CI passes.
Review: fresh Claude Code review (Opus, subagent), review-task skill

Source: DX-07 in `docs/driver-experience-plan.md`, plus DX-05's "reduce the upload card height".

## Objective

On a phone, the import card says "Drop your screenshot here · or click to browse your files": there is nothing to
drop and nothing to click. The card is 260px tall before the review form even starts. And nothing says which Flex
screens import, so a driver may try an empty offers list.

## Files to read

- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/templates/shifts.html`: the import panel (`#dropZone`, `#screenshotInput`).
- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/static/css/styles.css`: `.drop-zone`, the `max-width: 900px` layout.
- `flexbuddy/src/main/resources/static/js/app.js`: `processScreenshot` (PNG/JPEG, 5 MB), the drop handlers.
- Catalog keys: `import.uploadAScreenshot`, `import.chooseAPngOrJpeg`, `import.dropYourScreenshotHere`,
  `import.orClickToBrowseYour`.

## Required behavior

1. **Touch wording.** On touch devices (`(hover: none) and (pointer: coarse)`) the card reads "Choose a screenshot" /
   "Tap to pick one from your photos" (new `import.chooseAScreenshot`, `import.tapToPickFromPhotos`). Other devices
   keep "Drop your screenshot here" / "or click to browse your files". The hidden wording is `display: none`, so the
   button's accessible name is only the visible text.
2. **Shorter card.** In the phone layout (`max-width: 900px`) the card is at most about 150px tall (from 260px), with
   the same 44px-plus tap area and the dashed outline.
3. **Which screens work.** Under the card, two short lines (`field-hint` style): "Works with a Schedule Details
   screenshot of a block you accepted, or an Earnings screenshot of a block you worked." and "An offers list with no
   block selected can't be imported." (new `import.worksWith`, `import.notOffersList`). No real screenshots are
   shown.
4. The format and size line ("PNG or JPEG up to 5 MB") stays: it matches `processScreenshot` and the server.
   Drag-and-drop, the file chooser, keyboard activation and the shared-screenshot path are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Touch devices see tap wording; a mouse or trackpad sees drop/click wording; desktop drag-and-drop still works.
- [ ] The phone card is shorter; Step 2 starts higher; no horizontal scrolling at 360/390/430px.
- [ ] The supported-screens lines distinguish an accepted block (schedule) from a worked block (earnings) and rule
      out an empty offers list.
- [ ] The chooser stays a keyboard-operable button; English and Spanish.

## Validation

- `.\scripts\verify.ps1 -TaskId FB-020`; a `PageControllerTest` assertion that both wordings and the guidance render.
- Browser: 390px with the mobile preset (touch) and at desktop width (mouse); card height before/after; Spanish.

## Changed after review

- The offers line reads "An empty Offers screen has no block to import." The parser reads offer cards, so only an
  empty Offers screen has nothing to import (FB-020 review). Spanish uses "Detalles de la programación", like the
  catalog's name for Schedule.
