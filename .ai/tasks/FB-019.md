## Measured before (local test account, 390x844, signed in)

- Header 88px; bottom navigation from y 762; the floating + (`#quickActionButton`) at y 684-740, x 318-374, over the
  right edge of every full-width field it passes.
- Schedule with nothing upcoming: "Next up · No upcoming blocks" (151px), then an empty "Next 14 days" panel (198px)
  saying the same thing; the calendar starts at y 565.
- Expenses: the form panel (663px) comes first; the history panel starts at y 969 and its first row at y 1480, below
  two cost notes, the filters (141px) and the category breakdown (154px): over two screens of scrolling.

## Required behavior

1. **Schedule.** When there is no next block and nothing in the next 14 days, the empty "Next 14 days" panel is hidden
   (Next up already says so), so the calendar moves up. It comes back as soon as anything is upcoming. The rule is an
   exported `showsUpcomingPanel(upcoming)` in `schedule.js`.
2. **Expenses.** On phones (the existing `max-width: 900px` layout) the Expenses title row has a "Go to expense
   history" text button that scrolls the history panel into view under the header and moves focus to its heading.
   Hidden on wider layouts, where both panels sit side by side.
3. **Fixed controls.** On phones, while a text-like input, select or textarea has focus, the floating + and the bottom
   navigation are hidden, so they can't cover the field, its validation message or the keyboard's action row. They
   come back when focus leaves the field. The rule is an exported, pure `hidesFixedControls(element)` in a new
   `static/js/field-focus.js` (checkbox, radio, range, file, button-like inputs and buttons don't count).
4. Desktop layout, tap targets, modal focus and back/cancel behavior are unchanged. No new dependency.

## Out of scope

- Import screen and upload card (FB-020), Home, Reports, Account; header height.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Empty Schedule reaches the calendar sooner; with any upcoming block the panel is shown as before.
- [ ] Expenses has a direct route to history on phones; keyboard users land on the history heading.
- [ ] While a field is focused on a phone, nothing fixed covers it; leaving the field restores the controls.
- [ ] 360, 390 and 430px widths, landscape, and Spanish wrap without horizontal scrolling.
- [ ] English and Spanish text.
