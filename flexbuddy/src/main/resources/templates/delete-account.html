ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your account inside FlexBuddy. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to FlexBuddy and open Account.
  2. Download a backup if you want to keep a personal copy.
  3. Find Delete account, enter your current password, and type delete.
  4. Select Delete account permanently.

What FlexBuddy deletes

  • Your name, email address, password hash, and account settings
  • All shifts and expenses, including items in Recently deleted
  • Reminder history, calendar links, and push-notification subscriptions
  • Persistent sign-in tokens for every device

Uploaded screenshots are processed in memory and are never stored. Backup files already downloaded to your device remain under your control because FlexBuddy does not store them.
